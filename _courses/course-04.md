## About this course

Marketing Research focuses on how research can be used to provide relevant and accurate information for marketing decisions. The course teaches us how to identify a business problem, design an appropriate research study, collect and analyze data, and use the results to make recommendations. One of the things that makes this course stand out is that we get to apply what we learn to a real business client. I am taking this course as part of my Digital Marketing Strategy minor, but I also think the skills connect really well with my Communication major. I like being able to take research concepts and apply them to a real client.

## About Professor Name

Steve Matuszak is an Assistant Professor of Practice in Virginia Tech's Marketing Department. His academic background combines business and communication. 

he earned a master's degree in Business Administration and a master's degree in Communication from Virginia Tech, as well as bachelor's degrees in Communication and Theatre from Miami University of Ohio. Before teaching, he gained professional experience in areas including public relations, sales, training and development, consulting, and corporate training. He also previously served as Executive Director of a Chicago-based corporate training and consulting company that worked with Fortune 500 and international clients.

### Research interests

Professor Matuszak is an Assistant Professor of Practice rather than a research professor, but he does conduct research related to business education and communication. His interests include communication apprehension and other challenges that can prevent people from developing and demonstrating effective communication skills. He is also interested in corporate training and development.

### Publications

As an Assistant Professor of Practice, Professor Matuszak's faculty profile emphasizes applied research, teaching, and professional projects rather than listing traditional academic publications.

### Service and outreach

Professor Matuszak is involved in several service and outreach efforts at Virginia Tech and within the Pamplin College of Business. He serves as chair of Pamplin's Welcoming Committee, participates in its Assessment of Learning Committee, and is involved in business communication skills education. He also works with Virginia Tech's Digital Assessment Tools Community of Practice to help develop tools that allow instructors to better understand student learning.

He also helps lead Pamplin Marketing's partnerships with the Virginia Economic Development Partnership (VEDP) and Virginia International Trade Alliance (VITAL). Through these partnerships, his student teams provide marketing research and support to Virginia companies interested in international expansion.
